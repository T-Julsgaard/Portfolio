## v81: gaze-aware timelines and quiet Volunteering

## Stop info display: assemble mode, facets, covers, buttons, READ MORE (v23-v46)

**Stop info display** (v23, reworked v26–v27 — dev tools > "Stop Info" dropdown): `assemble` (default) or `panel` (the classic HTML overlay + clamped mouse-look, kept intact). In assemble mode a docked stop is a panorama of **facets** (text blocks / ASCII covers) hung at fixed yaws around the camera, **all at eye level** (v37 — anchor z = the camera's own z, pitch 0, so content sits where the scene is and the visitor never cranes); the visitor has free 360° drag-look (`freeYaw`/`freePitch`, street-view drag; scroll still departs, so panel-mode's drag-to-depart doesn't exist here). Each facet fires/releases on **yaw alone** (v37 — horizontal slices: fires within `F_GAZE_IN` ~23° of its compass direction, releases past `F_GAZE_OUT` ~35°; pitch never gates, so looking up or down cannot miss or drop content — with 360/7 ≈ 51.4° gallery spacing the ±23° fire windows can't overlap): its recruits fly staggered quadratic-bezier arcs, swap geometry mid-flight (shared `glyphGeo` cache), and settle as world-anchored content; **every other glyph in that facet's sight corridor is absorbed too** (flies into a random letter, fading only as it lands) — so content reads on pure black, and since v43 **nothing merely fades in place** (fade-in-place survives only as a budget-overflow fallback the raised budget makes unreachable). Partitioning happens once per dock (`assembleStop`): items exclusive per facet (nearest to its anchor, reading-order paired); **every** facet whose patch contains a glyph gets its own absorb record for it (v43 — was: corridor leftovers to the angularly nearest facet only) — gaze changes just replay cached flights, no per-frame searches. **The whole corridor joins the assembly (v40)**: pass 3 records EVERY glyph inside a facet's ellipse — other facets' owned letters included — for absorb flights staggered over the same window as the letter flights (budget `F_EXTRA_MAX` 9000 since v43, raised past the measured worst case ~7.2k members per corridor, validated against the real `asciiData`, so overflow never happens in practice). The near-field gate is `v.dot(F.dir)<0.2` (v43; was 1.2 in v41, 4 before) and each glyph's rim widens with proximity (`×(1+(10−vlen)·0.06)` under 10 units). **The sight cone (v43 — replaces scrapped v42's whole-foreground clearing; `v42.html` was deleted, its number stays burned)**: any glyph nearer than the content plane (`depth<dist+2`) and inside the content block's *exact angular footprint* (`coneY/coneP` = block half-extent + 0.06 rad — catches the rectangle's corners, which the inscribed ellipse missed, and glyphs right at the POV) always gets an absorb record, budget-exempt, on every facet type — nothing sits still or fades between the visitor and the text/image/buttons, while the background beyond the content is untouched (v42's mistake was clearing the whole ±60°/±49° facing view). **Facet-to-facet handoff (v43)**: absorb records are self-clocked (`rec.st`/`rec.mode`, animated in every non-`home` state, so a *holding* facet can still receive) and `m.userData.hold` names the facet currently animating a glyph, arbitrating every conflict. Because the gaze hysteresis makes a neighbor fire *before* the current facet releases, `facetOut` looks each departing glyph up in `stopFacets.byMesh` and, if an active facet has a record for it, launches that record (`launchAbsorb`) instead of the home flight — the old facet's letters fly *straight into* the new content instead of returning home and fading (items flag `handed` so their stale home flight can never resume). `facetIn` conversely steals from an `'out'`-state owner/holder mid-flight, and owners always reclaim their own letters (`facetIn` items clear `hold`). Departure (`disassemble`) passes `noHand=true` so everything genuinely flies home. Handed-off letters morph back to home geometry+color mid-flight; the receiver's landing/restore paths fully restore geometry (`rec.g0`), color and `userData.asm`, so a glyph can bounce A→B→A indefinitely. Ordinary stops have one facet dead ahead (auto-assembles on dock; layout via `buildStopLayout`). **v37 removed the whole facet-height machinery** — the per-direction needed-height (terrain clearance), the ~24° elevation cap and the gallery's shared ring height are gone; every anchor is simply eye level, and the elliptical clearing already guarantees legibility over any terrain. **Arrivals are continuous (v40, sealed v41)**: the flight itself blends the down-gaze up to the horizon over its last 30% (`levelQuat` slerp in the flying branch; the entering branch levels its target outright — assemble mode only, panel mode keeps the raw `poseAt` gaze), and `assembleStop` sets `freePitch=freePitchT=-pitch0` so the docked camera math starts exactly where the flight ended. `frame()` also caps dt at 50 ms: the gallery dock's heavy partition used to inflate one frame's dt and make the old arrival ease consume ~90% of its travel in a single step (the v39 "arrival snap"). v41 removed the last residual cut-over: `dock()` captures the exact touchdown pose (`dockT0`/`dockPos`/`dockQuat`) and the docked branch eases from it into its own pose math over 1.6 s (`dr` lerp/slerp) — so the flight's damped-follow lag, the `lookTX/lookTY` mouse sway and the idle breathing drift all fade in instead of switching on at full amplitude at flight end. (A dev-tools re-assemble while already docked sees `dr`=1, i.e. no blend.) The **clearing is elliptical, not rectangular** (v27): each facet fades/absorbs every glyph inside a jittered angular ellipse (`Ry/Rp` = block half-extent + 0.15 rad, per-glyph rim jitter + per-glyph fade rates via `fades[].k`), patches may overlap between facets (v43: every overlapping facet keeps its own absorb record; `userData.hold` decides who animates). Fade speed was retuned in v39 (`F_FADE_MS` 420→260, `k` floor 0.5→0.9) — the slowest glyphs used to linger visibly after the text had assembled. A stop with a `gallery:` array (the **Case studies** stop, waypoint xf-0.34/yf0.22) adds 6 ASCII covers ringing the visitor at **even 360/7 spacing** (earlier ±170° spread put buses/food only 20° apart), all level at eye height like every other facet (v37; the old shared max-zNeed ring is gone). `ASCII_COVERS` (~11KB) is generated **offline** by `gen_covers.py` (34-col grids, ramp `' .:-=+*#%@'`, bright paper covers auto-inverted so ink glows, cells colored by luminance via `setImgColor`). **v36 full-frame fill**: the three white-background covers (`vrchat`, `radiology`, `wegovy`) were regenerated so the *whole* frame is ASCII, not just the subject — earlier they mapped the white paper to spaces (empty), so the cover was subject-shaped and the develop into a rectangular photo looked wrong.
